## HEALTHY CREATIVES

- **O que é:** trabalho de conclusão de curso (TCC) do Eric em Design Gráfico,
  Centro Universitário Belas Artes de São Paulo, concluído em dezembro de 2025
- **Orientação:** Prof.ª Elis Souza
- **Publicado no LinkedIn:** 12/02/2026 (`datePublished` 2026-02-12T20:51:16Z)
- **Engajamento (29/09/2026):** 44 reações · 7 comentários
- **O que ele fez:** estratégia de marca, identidade visual e linha de embalagens

### Texto do post (original, em inglês)

> I have a major milestone to share! In December, I completed my Graphic Design
> degree at Centro Universitário Belas Artes de São Paulo and finalized my thesis
> project, Healthy Creatives. The project was born from a real concern. Creative
> professionals deal daily with stress, sedentary routines, and lack of focus.
> The proposal was to connect health, functional supplementation, and information
> design to support creative performance with clarity, responsibility, and no
> exaggerated promises. I developed the brand strategy, visual identity, and
> packaging line guided by one simple principle: less noise, more clarity. Less
> friction, more execution. Closing this chapter with a project so aligned with
> what I believe about the role of design was meaningful. I am grateful to my
> classmates for the exchanges throughout this journey and to my advisor, Prof.
> Elis Souza, for her guidance and support during the entire process. On to the
> next challenges 🚀
